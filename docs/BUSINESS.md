# Business Pillar — Loyalty SaaS MVP

## 1. Purpose

The Loyalty SaaS platform enables dental clinics to operate a configurable loyalty program for their patients.

The MVP consists of:

* Patient Application
* Admin Application
* Backend API
* Relational Database

The system allows patients to:

* earn loyalty points through dental services
* earn points through referrals
* track their loyalty progress
* unlock multiple rewards
* redeem available rewards
* view available dental services and their prices
* calculate how many loyalty points a service generates

Clinic administrators can:

* manage patients
* manage loyalty points and referrals
* configure loyalty rewards
* confirm reward redemption
* manage loyalty-related data
* view patient loyalty history

---

# 2. Core Loyalty Concepts

The loyalty program is based on two balances:

1. **Points**
2. **Referral Credits**

Both are relevant when determining whether a patient has unlocked a reward.

A patient can accumulate both through normal activity.

Rewards require points and referral credits according to their individual requirements. Redeeming (using) a reward consumes neither (§13).

---

# 3. Loyalty Points

## 3.1 Points from Services

Each clinic has its own **scoring configuration**, defining how many loyalty points are earned for an amount spent. It consists of two parameters:

* **spend amount** — an amount in RSD
* **points awarded** — the number of loyalty points earned for that spend amount

The scoring configuration belongs to exactly one clinic. It must never be shared between clinics or applied to another clinic's services.

The platform provides an initial **default** scoring configuration for every new clinic:

**Default: 100 RSD = 1,000 loyalty points** (spend amount 100 RSD, points awarded 1,000)

This is a default, not a mandatory ratio. The Clinic Admin can review and change the clinic's scoring configuration during clinic setup, subject to the application's authorization rules.

With the default scoring configuration:

| Service amount | Loyalty points |
| -------------: | -------------: |
|        100 RSD |          1,000 |
|        500 RSD |          5,000 |
|      1,000 RSD |         10,000 |
|     10,000 RSD |        100,000 |
|    100,000 RSD |      1,000,000 |

The exact service price is defined by the clinic's service catalog.

The system calculates the points generated by a service based on its price and the scoring configuration of the clinic associated with the service transaction.

A service recorded before the patient has registered and linked their user account to their Patient record generates no loyalty points (§5.1).

## 3.2 Rounding of Calculated Points

Points before rounding are:

```text
points before rounding = service amount in RSD / spend amount × points awarded
```

With the default scoring configuration this is `service amount in RSD × 10`, the same as `amount / 100 × 1,000` (§16).

The result is rounded to the nearest whole number of points using **midpoint rounding away from zero**:

* a fractional part of **0.5 or greater** rounds **up**
* a fractional part **below 0.5** rounds **down**

The rounded result is the number of points generated by the service (the **calculated points**).

Rounding applies only to the calculated points. The service amount itself is not rounded.

Examples, using the default scoring configuration:

| Service amount | Before rounding | Loyalty points |
| -------------: | --------------: | -------------: |
|    1,234.54 RSD |       12,345.4 |         12,345 |
|    1,234.55 RSD |       12,345.5 |         12,346 |
|    1,234.56 RSD |       12,345.6 |         12,346 |
|    7,500.00 RSD |       75,000.0 |         75,000 |

## 3.3 Maximum Points Balance

A patient's points balance must never exceed **1,000,000 points**.

If an earning operation would take the balance above 1,000,000 points:

* only the points that fit within the remaining balance are credited
* the excess points are discarded and are not carried forward
* the earning operation itself is not rejected because of the cap

This applies to every earning operation, including service points (§3.1) and referral rewards (§4.2).

The system therefore distinguishes:

* **calculated points** — the points an operation generates (for a service, the rounded result of §3.2)
* **credited points** — the points actually added to the balance after the cap is applied

Example:

```text
Current balance:     950,000
Calculated points:   120,000
Credited points:      50,000
New balance:       1,000,000
Discarded:            70,000
```

The loyalty transaction for an earning operation records the credited points (§14).

Redeeming a reward does not reduce the points balance (§13).

# 4. Referral System

## 4.1 Referral Qualification

A referral becomes valid when:

1. An existing patient refers a new patient.
2. The referred person registers/enters the loyalty system through the referral mechanism.
3. The new patient comes to the dental clinic.
4. The new patient completes a dental examination.

The referred patient does **not** need to purchase or use a dental service for the referral to become valid.

A valid referral generates referral rewards only when the registration and account-linking requirement in §4.2 is also met.

---

## 4.2 Referral Rewards

For every valid referral that meets the registration and account-linking requirement below:

* Referring patient receives **50,000 points**
* Referred patient receives **50,000 points**

Example:

```text
Referral #1
Referrer       +50,000 points
New patient    +50,000 points
```

**Registration and account-linking requirement.** A valid referral generates referral rewards only if **both** the referring patient and the referred patient have already registered in the Patient App and linked their user accounts to their respective Patient records (§5.1) at the moment the referral becomes valid (§4.1).

If either patient has not registered and linked their account at that moment:

* neither patient receives the 50,000-point referral reward
* the referral generates no referral credits and no referral progress for either patient
* the reward is permanently forfeited for that referral; registering or linking an account later does not grant it retroactively

The referral and the related visit remain in the historical records, but they do not create loyalty benefits, then or later.

A patient can receive a maximum of **5 referral rewards** through the referral program.

Therefore, the maximum points obtainable through referrals for one patient are:

**5 × 50,000 = 250,000 points**

The remaining points required to reach the maximum progress are earned through dental services.

# 5. Loyalty Progress

The Patient App displays loyalty progress from:

**0 → 1,000,000 points**

The maximum progress value is intentionally represented as **1,000,000 points** to make the loyalty target visually clear to the patient.

Example:

```text
750,000 / 1,000,000 points
75%
```

The progress bar must visually communicate:

* current points
* maximum points
* available milestones
* locked milestones
* achieved milestones
* redeemed rewards where applicable

## 5.1 Loyalty Cycle

Each patient has an individual loyalty cycle.

The first cycle starts when the patient registers in the Patient App and their user account is linked to their Patient record. A cycle lasts one year.

**Loyalty eligibility before registration.** A patient cannot earn or have loyalty points, referral credits, referral progress, rewards, or any other loyalty-program benefits before successfully registering in the Patient App and linking their user account to their Patient record. The first cycle starts at the moment registration and linking are successfully completed.

Services, visits and referral activity recorded before that moment do not generate loyalty points, referral credits, referral progress or rewards, and must not be credited retroactively to the first cycle. A referral generates rewards only if both the referring and the referred patient meet this requirement when the referral becomes valid (§4.2).

A cycle ends in exactly one of two ways:

* **successful completion** (§5.2), or
* **expiry** (§5.3).

Each subsequent cycle starts on the actual end date of the previous cycle, whether it ended by successful completion or by expiry.

## 5.2 Successful Completion

A cycle is successfully completed when the patient has satisfied all of the following conditions:

1. The patient has reached the 1,000,000-point target during the cycle.
2. The patient has completed five successful referrals during the cycle.
3. The patient has redeemed (used) every reward they unlocked during the cycle (§13).

When all three conditions are met, the system completes the cycle automatically, as part of the operation that satisfies the last condition (for example, recording the redemption of the last unlocked reward). Clinic Admin approval is not required.

The completion and its timestamp must be recorded so that they are auditable.

## 5.3 Expiry

A cycle that has not been successfully completed expires one year after its start date, regardless of the patient's progress. This includes a patient who has not reached the points target, has not completed five referrals, has not unlocked all rewards, or has not redeemed any unlocked reward.

Expiry is not a successful completion. An expired cycle must never be recorded or presented as successfully completed (for example, as a completed Platinum cycle).

The expiry and its timestamp must be recorded so that they are auditable.

## 5.4 Starting a New Cycle

When a cycle ends, by successful completion or by expiry, active cycle progress is reset and the patient must start again. At the start of the new cycle:

* The active points balance resets to zero.
* Referral progress resets to zero.
* Reward progression and availability are recalculated for the new cycle.

The system must record how the previous cycle ended (successful completion or expiry), when it ended, and the reset of the active progress, so that the end of the cycle and the reset can be explained from loyalty history (§14).

Historical service records, loyalty transactions, referral records, reward records and reward-redemption (usage) records are preserved for audit and reporting. Resetting a cycle must never delete historical records.

# 6. Tiers

The loyalty program consists of three primary tiers.

## Tier 1

Requirement:

* Minimum **1 valid referral**
* Minimum **300,000 points**

Tier 1 rewards include:

| Required referrals | Required points | Reward                         |
| -----------------: | --------------: | ------------------------------ |
|                  1 |         300,000 | Peskiranje                     |
|                  1 |         400,000 | Uklanjanje kamenca + poliranje |
|                  1 |         500,000 | Kompozitni ispun               |

Each reward is an independent reward.

A patient does not need to redeem one reward before becoming eligible for another reward.

---

# 7. Tier 2

Requirement:

* Minimum **3 valid referrals**
* Minimum **600,000 points**

Tier 2 rewards include:

| Required referrals | Required points | Reward                                      |
| -----------------: | --------------: | ------------------------------------------- |
|                  3 |         600,000 | 15% popusta na fiksnu protezu               |
|                  3 |         700,000 | 30% popusta na hiruršku ekstrakciju umnjaka |

Each reward is independently evaluated against the patient's current loyalty balance.

---

# 8. Tier 3

Requirement:

* Minimum **5 valid referrals**
* **1,000,000 points**

Tier 3 reward:

| Required referrals | Required points | Reward                  |
| -----------------: | --------------: | ----------------------- |
|                  5 |       1,000,000 | Kućno izbeljivanje zuba |

This represents the maximum loyalty progress in the MVP.

# 9. Milestones

The loyalty progress bar contains visual milestones.

Each milestone represents a reward or important loyalty threshold.

Milestones may contain:

* point threshold
* referral requirement
* reward name
* reward description
* icon
* tier
* status

Possible milestone statuses:

* Locked
* Available — the reward is unlocked (§13.1)
* Redeemed — the reward has been used (§13.1)

The Patient App should visually represent milestones using appropriate dental/loyalty icons, such as a tooth icon or another reward-related icon.

Milestones must be driven by backend data rather than being hardcoded exclusively in the frontend.

---

# 10. Reward Eligibility

A reward is available only when **all of its requirements are satisfied**.

A reward may define:

* required points
* required referrals
* tier
* reward type
* reward description
* associated service, when applicable

Example:

```text
Reward:
Kompozitni ispun

Required points:
500,000

Required referrals:
1
```

A patient with:

```text
600,000 points
1 referral
```

is eligible.

A patient with:

```text
600,000 points
0 referrals
```

is not eligible.

A patient with:

```text
400,000 points
2 referrals
```

is also not eligible.

Both conditions must be satisfied.

---

# 11. Independent Rewards

Rewards are **not dependent on reaching the final Tier 3 reward**.

A patient can redeem a reward as soon as the requirements for that specific reward are satisfied.

For example:

```text
1 referral
300,000 points
```

allows the patient to redeem the Tier 1 300,000-point reward.

The patient does not have to wait until:

```text
5 referrals
1,000,000 points
```

---

# 12. Combining Rewards

A patient can accumulate enough points and referrals to become eligible for multiple rewards.

The system must evaluate each reward independently against the patient's current loyalty balance.

For example, a patient may have several rewards available simultaneously.

The patient may redeem an available reward without having to redeem the other available rewards first.

Redeeming a reward does not consume points or referral credits (§13). Redeeming one reward therefore never causes another reward to become unavailable.

---

# 13. Reward Redemption

## 13.1 Redemption Rule

In this document, **redeeming** a reward means the patient actually **using** a reward they have unlocked. Unlocking and redeeming are distinct:

* a reward is **unlocked** (earned) when the patient satisfies all of its requirements (§10); it is then shown as *Available*
* a reward is **redeemed** (used) when the Clinic Admin confirms and records that the patient actually used it (§19)

A patient can redeem only rewards they have unlocked. An unlocked reward cannot be exchanged for a different service from the clinic's service catalog (§15).

The patient chooses when to redeem an unlocked reward: as soon as it is unlocked, or later, after unlocking more rewards.

When a reward is redeemed:

* the patient's points balance is not changed
* the patient's referral credits are not changed
* the redemption is recorded in loyalty history, including its date/time and the Clinic Admin responsible (§14)
* the reward redemption is associated with the patient
* the Clinic Admin confirms/records the redemption

The system must record the redemption, and any cycle completion it causes (§5.2), as one atomic business operation.

---

## 13.2 Example

Patient currently has:

```text
Points:       750,000
Referrals:    3
```

Patient redeems:

```text
Reward:       Tier 1 reward
Requirement:  300,000 points + 1 referral
```

After redemption:

```text
Points:       750,000
Referrals:    3
```

The reward is recorded as redeemed.

The patient's balances are unchanged, so the other rewards the patient has unlocked remain unlocked.

---

# 14. Redemption History

The system must maintain a history of loyalty transactions.

Examples of transaction types:

* ServicePointsEarned
* ReferralPointsEarned
* RewardRedeemed
* ManualPointsAdjustment
* ManualReferralAdjustment

A transaction should contain enough information to establish:

* patient
* transaction type
* points change
* referral change
* related reward, when applicable
* related service, when applicable
* date/time
* user/admin responsible for the operation where applicable

For an earning operation, the points change is the number of **credited points** actually added to the balance, not the calculated points before the maximum balance is applied (§3.3).

For a reward redemption, the points change and the referral change are zero (§13).

The end of a loyalty cycle and the resulting reset of active progress must also be recorded in loyalty history (§5.4).

The transaction history provides an audit trail and prevents loyalty balances from being treated as unexplained mutable numbers.

# 16. Points Calculator

The Patient App must contain a points calculator.

The patient can enter a service amount and see how many loyalty points that amount generates.

Example:

```text
Amount:
7,500 RSD

Calculated points:
75,000
```

The calculation uses the scoring configuration of the patient's clinic (§3.1):

```text
points = amount / spend amount × points awarded
```

With the default scoring configuration this is:

```text
points = amount / 100 × 1,000
```

The result is rounded to a whole number of points using midpoint rounding away from zero, exactly as defined in §3.2, so the calculator shows the same calculated points the service generates.

The calculator shows calculated points. The points actually credited can be lower if the patient's balance would exceed the 1,000,000-point maximum (§3.3).

The calculator should help patients understand how different services contribute to their loyalty progress.

Where appropriate, the UI may also display:

* current points
* calculated points
* resulting points
* remaining points until the next milestone

# 18. Admin Application — Loyalty Management

Clinic Admin must be able to:

* view patient loyalty balance
* view points
* view referral count
* view loyalty transactions
* view available rewards
* view redeemed rewards
* confirm reward redemption
* manage loyalty configuration, including the clinic's scoring configuration (§3.1)
* manage services and prices
* manage reward definitions

The Admin Application is responsible for operational control of the loyalty program.

---

# 19. Clinic Admin Reward Redemption

The Clinic Admin is responsible for confirming that a reward has actually been used.

The redemption operation must:

1. verify that the patient satisfies the reward requirements
2. verify that the reward can be redeemed
3. create the redemption transaction, recording its date/time and the responsible Clinic Admin
4. mark the reward redemption in the patient's loyalty history
5. complete the cycle if this redemption satisfies the last completion condition (§5.2)

The redemption does not deduct points or referral credits.

The operation must be atomic.

If any part fails, the entire redemption must fail and the patient's loyalty state must remain unchanged.

---

# 20. Loyalty Balance Integrity

The system must prevent:

* negative point balances
* point balances above 1,000,000 points (§3.3)
* negative referral balances
* redemption of a reward whose point requirement is not satisfied
* redemption of a reward whose referral requirement is not satisfied
* redemption of an unavailable reward
* exceeding the maximum referral reward count
* unauthorized modification of another clinic's loyalty data

All loyalty-changing operations must be validated by the backend.

The frontend must never be considered the source of truth for loyalty calculations.

---

# 21. Multi-Tenant Business Rule

Each clinic represents an independent tenant.

A clinic can manage only its own:

* patients
* services
* rewards
* loyalty configuration, including its scoring configuration
* loyalty transactions
* redemptions

A patient belonging to Clinic A must not be able to access Clinic B's loyalty data.

All business operations must respect tenant boundaries.

---

# 22. Business Rules vs Configuration

The following are core MVP business rules:

* service points are calculated using the scoring configuration of the clinic (§3.1)
* calculated points are rounded to a whole number using midpoint rounding away from zero (§3.2)
* maximum progress = 1,000,000 points
* the points balance never exceeds 1,000,000 points; points beyond it are discarded, not carried forward (§3.3)
* no loyalty points, referral credits, referral progress, rewards or other loyalty benefits before the patient registers and links their user account to their Patient record; earlier activity is not credited retroactively (§5.1)
* each patient has an individual one-year loyalty cycle that is completed automatically when its points, referral and reward-usage conditions are all met, and otherwise expires after one year; expiry is not a successful completion; the next cycle starts on the actual end date and resets the active state without deleting history (§5.1–§5.4)
* referral = valid after examination
* referral reward = 50,000 points for both patients, only if both are registered and linked when the referral becomes valid; otherwise neither receives it and it is permanently forfeited (§4.2)
* maximum referral rewards = 5
* rewards require both points and referrals
* redemption (reward usage) consumes neither points nor referral credits (§13)
* multiple rewards can be independently available
* Clinic Admin confirms redemption

The following should be configurable where practical:

* scoring configuration per clinic: spend amount (RSD) and points awarded (§3.1; default 100 RSD = 1,000 points)
* service prices
* service catalog
* reward names
* reward descriptions
* reward icons
* reward point requirements
* reward referral requirements
* reward tier
* reward availability

This allows the loyalty engine to remain reusable for different dental clinics.

# 23. Business Source of Truth

The Business Pillar defines the expected behavior of the MVP.

Implementation must follow these rules.

If a technical implementation conflicts with a business rule, the business rule takes precedence and the technical design should be adapted.

The backend is the authoritative source for:

* points
* referrals
* reward eligibility
* reward redemption
* loyalty calculations
* loyalty history

The frontend is responsible for presenting this information and providing the user interaction layer.

# 24. MVP Scope Boundary

The MVP does not require:

* complex gamification
* leaderboards
* social feeds
* push notification infrastructure
* advanced AI functionality
* complex campaign management
* multiple loyalty currencies
* subscription billing

These may be considered future extensions.

The MVP focuses on a reliable, understandable dental loyalty system with:

**Patients → Services → Points → Referrals → Milestones → Rewards → Redemption → History**

# 25. Core Loyalty Flow

The complete MVP flow is:

```text
Patient
   │
   ├── Uses dental service
   │       ↓
   │   Earns points
   │
   ├── Refers new patient
   │       ↓
   │   New patient completes examination
   │       ↓
   │   Both patients receive 50,000 points
   │   (only if both are registered and linked, §4.2)
   │
   ↓
Loyalty Balance
   │
   ├── Points
   └── Referrals
   │
   ↓
Reward Eligibility Engine
   │
   ├── Locked rewards
   ├── Available rewards
   └── Redeemed rewards
   │
   ↓
Patient selects/requests reward
   │
   ↓
Clinic Admin confirms redemption
   │
   ↓
Atomic Redemption Transaction
   │
   ├── Record redemption (no points or referrals deducted)
   └── Complete cycle if all completion conditions are met (§5.2)
   │
   ↓
Updated Loyalty State
```

# 26. Final MVP Loyalty Model

The fundamental business model is:

> **Patients earn points through dental services and valid referrals. Referrals also increase the patient's referral balance. A referral generates these benefits only if both patients are registered and linked when it becomes valid; otherwise it is permanently forfeited for both. Rewards have individual point and referral requirements. Once a patient satisfies a reward's requirements, that reward becomes available. Rewards can be redeemed independently and in combination with other rewards; redeeming a reward means using it and consumes neither points nor referral credits. Clinic Admin confirms redemption, and every loyalty change is recorded as a transaction. A cycle is completed automatically when its completion conditions are met, or otherwise expires after one year; either way the active progress resets for a new cycle.**

This model is the **Business Pillar source of truth for the MVP implementation**.
